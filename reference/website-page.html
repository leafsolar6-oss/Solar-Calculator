How do I find the watt? ▾

Check the sticker or nameplate on the appliance (usually at the back or bottom) — it shows a number followed by “W” or “Watts”. For ACs and motors, use the rated input watts, not the HP. If it only shows amps, multiply amps × voltage (e.g. 1.0A × 230V ≈ 230W).
